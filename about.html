About the Author & Digital Drift

Welcome to Digital Drift. This platform was born from a simple conviction: learning modern backend development, software architecture, and artificial intelligence should be intuitive, practical, and grounded in real-world engineering.

I'm Dhiraj Roy, a passionate backend developer and technology enthusiast. Over years of building production web services, APIs, database infrastructures, and cloud systems, I've seen how easy it is to get bogged down by incomplete documentation, obsolete tutorials, and theoretical complexity.

Here at Digital Drift, every article is written with code you can copy, run, and scale. Whether you are learning Spring Boot fundamentals, mastering PostgreSQL query indexing, configuring JWT authentication, or deploying containerized applications to Linux VPS servers, you will find comprehensive, step-by-step guidance.
